Fed Tone Tracker

An AI agent that reads every Federal Reserve speech and scores how hawkish or dovish it sounds, with the exact quote from the speech behind each score.

What we're trying to achieve

Between policy meetings, Fed officials give dozens of speeches. Markets move on small shifts in their tone: a governor leaning toward holding rates higher for longer, or warming up to cuts. Reading every speech takes hours, and headlines often miss the nuance.

The goal of this agent is a consistent, daily, evidence-backed read of where each official stands on monetary policy, so you can see at a glance how the Fed's tone is drifting over time.

  • Grounded in the speechEvery score comes with a quote. The agent checks the quote word for word against the speech and rejects any it can't find.
  • ConsistentOne rubric, one model, every speech, so scores can be compared across speakers and over time.
  • OpenThe code, prompt and data are public on GitHub, so you can see exactly how each call was made.

How the agent works

  1. WatchOnce a day, check the Federal Reserve's speech feed for anything new.
  2. ScreenSkip speeches that clearly aren't about monetary policy, like bank regulation or financial literacy talks.
  3. ReadPull the full text of the speech from federalreserve.gov.
  4. ScoreA language model rates the speaker's policy stance from −2 (strongly dovish) to +2 (strongly hawkish) using a fixed rubric, and picks a supporting quote.
  5. VerifyCheck that the quote really appears in the speech, sentence by sentence. If it doesn't, the result is thrown out.
  6. PublishSave the result and update this page automatically.
